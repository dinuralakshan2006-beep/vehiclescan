Connection and safety notes

  • This prototype supports common BLE UART-style adapters using Nordic UART (NUS), FFE0/FFE1 or FFF0/FFF1 services. Many ELM327 adapters use Classic Bluetooth or a proprietary profile and will not connect here.
  • Web Bluetooth requires HTTPS and is not supported by every browser. iPhone Safari/Chrome generally cannot connect to BLE from a webpage; use a supported Android browser or a compatible iOS BLE browser such as Bluefy, if available.
  • OBD-II generic data is not a full professional scan. ABS, airbag, transmission and manufacturer-specific modules may require dedicated equipment/software.
  • Never work under a vehicle supported only by a jack. Keep the car parked, parking brake on, and follow adapter/vehicle instructions.
VehicleScan is an independent demo tool. It does not guarantee fault interpretation, vehicle compatibility, or repair advice. Verify important results with a qualified technician.
